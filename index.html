Sevenfold: Privacy Policy

Effective 15 November 2026 · App: Sevenfold: Hex Merge Puzzle (com.atomilovskyi.sevenfold) · Developer: Andrii Tomilovskyi

In short: Sevenfold does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data. The game works fully offline and does not have internet access.

What the app stores

To keep your progress, the game saves these things only on your device, inside the app's own storage:

  • your scores, best results and their dates, and game statistics;
  • earned badges and the state of an unfinished game;
  • your settings, such as language, background, sound and text size.

This information never leaves your device. It is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

What the app does not do

  • It does not ask for any device permissions and has no internet access.
  • It does not use accounts, sign-in, advertising, analytics, crash reporting or tracking.
  • It does not use the Advertising ID or collect device identifiers, location, contacts or files.
  • It does not sell or share any data with third parties.

Sharing your score

If you tap Share, the app hands a short text with your score to the Android share menu. You choose where it goes, and that app's own privacy policy applies. Sevenfold does not see or keep what you send.

Google Play

The app is distributed through Google Play. Google may process data about downloads and purchases under the Google Privacy Policy. If optional tips or other purchases are added in the future, they will be handled by Google Play billing, and this policy will be updated before that happens.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
