Sevenfold: Privacy Policy

Effective 19 November 2026 · App: Sevenfold: Hex Merge Puzzle (com.atomilovskyi.sevenfold) · Developer: Andrii Tomilovskyi, Ukraine

In short: Sevenfold has no accounts and never asks for your name, email or contacts. It sends anonymous gameplay statistics to Google Analytics for Firebase so we can make the game better, and you can turn this off in Settings at any time. Optional tips are paid through Google Play; we never see your card details. We don't show ads and don't sell data.

Who is responsible

The app is made and published by Andrii Tomilovskyi, an individual developer in Ukraine ("we" in this policy). For any question about your data, write to andrex2091@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

The game saves these things only in the app's own storage on your phone:

  • your scores, best results and their dates, and game statistics;
  • earned badges and the state of an unfinished game;
  • your settings, such as language, background, sound and text size;
  • whether you have sent a tip, so your thank-you rewards show.

This data is not sent to us. If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include it in your device backup, which Google keeps encrypted; you control this in your phone's settings. The data is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

Gameplay statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To understand how people play and to make the game better, the app sends usage statistics to Google Analytics for Firebase, a service run by Google. We use them to answer questions like: do new players finish the tutorial, how long do games last, which tips help, and where do players get stuck.

What is sent:

  • Game events: tutorial steps, games started and finished (mode, score, number of moves, play time, bursts, longest chain, whether Hold or Undo was used), help cards shown or closed, badges earned, background and sound settings, the screens you open, and tips sent (which tier).
  • Usage collected automatically by Firebase: first launch, sessions, time spent in the app, app version, Android version, device model, language, and approximate location (country and region) worked out from your IP address. Google Analytics does not store IP addresses.
  • An app-instance ID: a random number Firebase creates for this installation of the app. It lets us count returning players without knowing who they are. It changes if you reinstall the app, clear its data, or use Delete my statistics.

What is not sent: your name, email, contacts, photos, files, precise location or the Advertising ID. Advertising features are turned off: the data is not used for ads, ad personalization or tracking you across other apps.

You can turn statistics off in Settings → Share anonymous stats. When it is off, the app sends nothing to Google Analytics.

Google processes this data for us as a service provider, under the Google Analytics for Firebase terms and the Google Privacy Policy. Google may process it in the United States and other countries, using the safeguards described in its terms. See also how Google uses information from apps that use its services. Statistics are kept for 14 months and then deleted automatically.

Tips (in-app purchases)

Tips are optional and handled entirely by Google Play. Google processes your payment under its own terms and privacy policy. We receive order details from Google, such as the order number, the item, the price, the date and your country, but never your card or bank details. We use them to deliver your thank-you rewards, handle refunds and keep the accounting records the law requires.

Email

If you write to us, for example through Send feedback in Settings, we receive your email address, your message and the app version in the subject line. We use them only to reply and to improve the game, and we delete the conversation on request.

Sharing your score

If you tap Share, the app hands a short text with your score to the Android share menu. You choose where it goes, and that app's privacy policy applies. Sevenfold does not see or keep what you send.

Why we may use this data

For people in the European Union, the United Kingdom and similar places: we use gameplay statistics based on our legitimate interest in understanding and improving the game, which you can object to at any time with the Settings switch. We use order details to perform our agreement with you and to meet legal obligations. We use email to answer you.

Who we share data with

We do not sell personal data and do not share it for advertising. Only Google receives data, as described above, as our service provider and as the operator of Google Play. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Data on your device: until you clear it or uninstall the app.
  • Gameplay statistics: 14 months, then deleted by Google.
  • Order details: as long as tax and accounting rules require.
  • Emails: until the conversation is over, or earlier if you ask.

Security

Data sent to Google Analytics travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). The app asks for no device permissions other than internet access.

Your rights

You can turn off or delete statistics in Settings, and you can delete all game data on your device by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it (see Delete your data below). Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict its use, and to complain to your data protection authority. Because the app has no accounts, we usually cannot link statistics to you. Write to andrex2091@gmail.com and we will help as far as we can.

Delete your data

The game has no accounts, so the statistics we receive are not linked to your name or email. You can still remove the link between them and your phone, or ask us for help:

  1. Stop sharing: Settings → Share anonymous stats → off. From then on, nothing is sent.
  2. Delete my statistics: Settings → Delete my statistics. This turns sharing off and erases the statistics data and the app-instance ID stored on your phone, so statistics sent before can no longer be linked to it. Statistics already sent are deleted by Google automatically after 14 months.
  3. Game data on your phone: clear the app's data in Android settings, or uninstall the app. This removes your scores, badges and settings.
  4. Anything else: write to andrex2091@gmail.com, for example to delete an email conversation or order details we are not required to keep. We reply within 30 days.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, write to us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date. If the change is significant, the app will also mention it.
